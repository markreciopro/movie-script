ACT I The Phantom Surge

INT. RESORT OPERATIONS CENTER — NIGHT

A wall of monitors bleeds blue light across an empty office. Three dashboards disagree with each other. A GENERAL MANAGER stares at an occupancy report that doesn't match the labor report, which doesn't match what she saw on the floor an hour ago.

GENERAL MANAGER
Front desk says we're slammed. Payroll says we're overstaffed. Somebody's lying — and it isn't the guests.

EXT. POOL DECK — GOLDEN HOUR

A wave of unbooked walk-ins crashes over the towel station. Housekeeping is three rooms behind. Nobody scheduled for this — because nothing in the PMS predicted it.

A PHANTOM SURGE — demand nobody saw coming, because the system that tracks occupancy has never once spoken to the system that tracks the people who serve it.

INT. EXECUTIVE BOARDROOM — DAY

Quarterly numbers on the screen. Overtime is up. Guest satisfaction is down half a point nobody can explain. The CFO taps his pen.

CFO
Every property has this data. Nobody has this story.

Somewhere between the PMS, the POS, and the HR system, the truth is hiding in plain sight. It will take something with sharper eyes to find it.

ACT II The Parrot's Diagnosis

EXT. SKY ABOVE THE RESORT — CONTINUOUS

A shape cuts through the haze over the property. Feathers of emerald and gold catch the last light. MAREC — the Hospitality Data Science Parrot — banks low over the roofline, and for the first time tonight, the noise starts to resolve into a signal.

MAREC (V.O.)
Brawk! You don't have a data problem. You have a story nobody's told yet. Let's read it together.

01 — DESCRIPTIVE

Descriptive Vision

INT. DATA ROOM — Marec pulls PMS, POS, and HR feeds into one frame. What is actually happening — not what three separate dashboards claim.

02 — DIAGNOSTIC

Diagnostic Pulse

The Phantom Surge has a heartbeat. Marec traces it — why the friction happens, where the labor variance actually leaks.

03 — PREDICTIVE

Predictive Echo-Locating

A call thrown into next season's booking pace comes back with an answer: what will happen at peak, at shoulder, at the next holiday weekend.

04 — PRESCRIPTIVE

Prescriptive Optimization

Not a report. An instruction. What to do about it — exact shifts, exact headcount, margins protected before the surge lands.

MONTAGE — THE TRUSTED PARTNER FRAMEWORK

  • — An NDA is signed before a single row of data moves.
  • — Read-only exports only. Nothing at the property is touched, nothing put at risk.
  • — CSV and Excel bridges hand data across without friction for risk-averse IT teams.
  • — Python, R, SQL, and Power BI pipelines start turning static exports into a living, breathing model.

One by one, the dashboards stop arguing with each other. On the operations floor, the GM watches shift patterns click into place, matched hour for hour against tomorrow's occupancy curve.

INT. OPERATIONS CENTER — LATER — NIGHT

GENERAL MANAGER
This is the first time all three systems have told me the same thing.

MAREC (V.O.)
Brawk! They were always saying it. Nobody had taught them to talk to each other.

The dashboards begin to change color — from warning red, to a slow, steady gold.

ACT III The Golden Ledger

INT. EXECUTIVE BOARDROOM — DAY

The same room as Act I. The same screen. But the story on it has changed. Staffing curves trace occupancy almost perfectly. Overtime has stopped bleeding. Every department is reading from the same page.

MAREC (V.O.)
Brawk! Dashboards glow gold. Margins rise. Guest experience thrives.

STEP 01

Discovery

A complimentary 20-minute call. Zero commitment — just the property's friction points, out loud.

STEP 02

Assessment

A sample of operational data becomes a data-readiness report and a short list of quick-win KPIs.

STEP 03

30-Day Pilot

A custom dashboard, a simulation model, and an executive briefing deck — proof before scale.

EXT. RESORT — POOL DECK — GOLDEN HOUR — LATER

The same towel station from Act I. This time, the staffing count matches the crowd before it arrives. Housekeeping finishes early. A guest closes her eyes in the sun, unaware of the model quietly working behind the scenes to keep it this way.

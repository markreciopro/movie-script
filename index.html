ACT I The Phantom Surge

INT. RESORT OPERATIONS CENTER — NIGHT

A wall of monitors bleeds blue light across an empty office. Three dashboards disagree with each other. A GENERAL MANAGER stares at an occupancy report that doesn't match the labor report, which doesn't match what she saw on the floor an hour ago.

GENERAL MANAGERFront desk says we're slammed. Payroll says we're overstaffed. Somebody's lying — and it isn't the guests.

EXT. POOL DECK — GOLDEN HOUR

A wave of unbooked walk-ins crashes over the towel station. Housekeeping is three rooms behind. Nobody scheduled for this — because nothing in the PMS predicted it.

A PHANTOM SURGE — demand nobody saw coming, because the system that tracks occupancy has never once spoken to the system that tracks the people who serve it.

INT. EXECUTIVE BOARDROOM — DAY

Quarterly numbers on the screen. Overtime is up. Guest satisfaction is down half a point nobody can explain. The CFO taps his pen.

CFOEvery property has this data. Nobody has this story.

Somewhere between the PMS, the POS, and the HR system, the truth is hiding in plain sight. It will take something with sharper eyes to find it.

ACT II The Parrot's Diagnosis

EXT. SKY ABOVE THE RESORT — CONTINUOUS

A shape cuts through the haze over the property. Feathers of emerald and gold catch the last light. MAREC — the Hospitality Data Science Parrot — banks low over the roofline, and for the first time tonight, the noise starts to resolve into a signal.

MAREC (V.O.)Brawk! You don't have a data problem. You have a story nobody's told yet. Let's read it together.

INT. HOUSEKEEPING CORRIDOR — DAWN

The Phantom Surge was never the whole story. Underneath it, Marec finds a second problem hiding in plain sight — THERMAL BLIND SPOTS: housekeeping bottlenecks, inventory leaking room by room, compliance gaps nobody flagged, labor ratios quietly out of true.

MAREC (V.O.)Brawk! One dashboard. Every blind spot. No more chasing shadows floor by floor.

Six spreadsheets nobody fully trusted become one pane of glass. The GM stops needing to walk the floor to know what's happening on it.

MONTAGE — THE TRUSTED PARTNER FRAMEWORK

  • 1 An NDA is signed before a single row of data moves.
  • 2 Read-only exports only. Nothing at the property is touched, nothing put at risk.
  • 3 CSV and Excel bridges hand data across without friction for risk-averse IT teams.
  • 4 Python, R, SQL, and Power BI pipelines turn static exports into a living model.

One by one, the dashboards stop arguing with each other. On the operations floor, the GM watches shift patterns click into place, matched hour for hour against tomorrow's occupancy curve.

INT. OPERATIONS CENTER — LATER — NIGHT

GENERAL MANAGERThis is the first time all three systems have told me the same thing.

MAREC (V.O.)Brawk! They were always saying it. Nobody had taught them to talk to each other.

The dashboards begin to change color — from warning red, to a slow, steady gold.

ACT III The Golden Ledger

INT. EXECUTIVE BOARDROOM — DAY

The same room as Act I. The same screen. But the story on it has changed. Staffing curves trace occupancy almost perfectly. Overtime has stopped bleeding. Every department is reading from the same page.

MAREC (V.O.)Brawk! Dashboards glow gold. Margins rise. Guest experience thrives.
